### 1. Purpose and Nature of the Service

Wingene VIDA is a reflective journaling, self-awareness, and personal growth application built upon the four pillars of the **VIDA Method**:
* **V (Values):** Discovering, clarifying, and reaffirming core ethical principles;
* **I (Imperfections):** Lucidity in observing mistakes, blind spots, and opportunities for moral growth;
* **D (Decisions):** Converting intentions and reflection into disciplined, conscious action;
* **A (Attention):** Exercising presence, mindful awareness, and sensory connection with the present moment.

### 2. Data Sovereignty, Local Storage, and Backups

User privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental design principles of Wingene VIDA:

* **Local Storage (*Offline-First*):** All your notes, journal entries, ratings, tags, and reflections are saved locally on your device. The developers do not operate proprietary servers to store, access, or inspect your private journal entries.
* **Optional Google Drive Sync:** The App offers an optional cloud backup feature executed directly between your device and your own personal Google Drive account in a dedicated, hidden application folder (*appDataFolder*). The developers have no access to your Google account or your files.
* **End-to-End Encryption and User Password:** If you choose to configure a Backup Password, your data is encrypted locally using the robust AES standard before being transferred to Google Drive.
  * **Important:** Your backup password is under **your sole responsibility**. We do not store, know, or manage this password. If you forget it, we cannot recover or decrypt your backup data.
* **User Responsibility for Data Safety:** Users are solely responsible for maintaining the physical security of their devices, setting passwords, and keeping regular backups.
