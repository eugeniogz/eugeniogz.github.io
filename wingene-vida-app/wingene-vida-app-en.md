# Wingene VIDA App

**User guide, practical documentation, and foundation for the VIDA Ethical-Existential Method.**

**Wingene VIDA** is a reflective journaling application designed for self-knowledge, ethical practice, and continuous personal development. Built around the four axes of the VIDA Method (**Values, Imperfections, Decisions, and Attention**), the app transforms daily experiences into a structured path of awareness and human evolution.

> For a quick visual overview, product showcase, and direct store links, visit the official [Wingene VIDA Landing Page](https://blog.wingene.com.br/wingene-vida-app/index-en.html).

## The Five-Stage Practical Journey

Far beyond a traditional digital notepad, Wingene VIDA serves as both a reflective mirror and a conduct guide through an ongoing five-stage cycle:

```
[ 1. Daily Journaling ] ──> [ 2. Link to Purposes ] ──> [ 3. AI Insights ]
                                                                 │
[ 5. Monthly Summary & Cycles ] <── [ 4. Periodic Guidance ] <───┘
```

### 1. Recording and Classifying Observations
* **Everyday Accounts:** Document events, reactions, dialogues, moods, and challenges without prior moralizing filters or self-punishment.
* **Classification into Pillars:** Each log is categorized under one of the method's core axes (or classified with AI assistance in the mobile app):
  * **V (Values):** Ethical principles, personal boundaries, consistency, and moral conduct.
  * **I (Imperfections):** Clear-eyed recognition of impulses, flaws, or reactivities to be corrected.
  * **D (Decisions):** Conscious, deliberate choices made in everyday dilemmas.
  * **A (Attention):** The practice of mindful presence, here-and-now focus, and environmental awareness.
* **Ethical-Existential AI Analysis (Mobile App):** On your phone, the Life Mentor (Gemini) anonymously reviews your entry and returns structured feedback: a reflective reflection, a philosophical synthesis, and key learning concepts.

### 2. Purpose and Goal Management
* **Define Purposes:** Establish life purposes and short-, medium-, and long-term goals (e.g., cultivating patience in family relationships, building intellectual discipline, maintaining emotional composure).
* **Link Observations:** Group and link daily logs to a specific purpose, translating abstract aspirations into tangible consistency over time.

### 3. Practical Insights & Trend Mapping
* **Actionable Suggestions:** Receive practical recommendations for the following day based on recurring patterns in your entries.
* **Recognized Progress:** The app highlights subtle behavioral shifts and positive evolution that daily routines often mask.
* **Trigger Identification:** Identifies recurring mental states, times of day, or circumstances that spark unintended reactions.

### 4. Periodic Guidance
* **Consolidation of Strengths:** Periodic overviews highlighting ethical alignment and constructive decisions.
* **Constructive Alerts:** Compassionate reminders regarding areas of imperfection that require continued vigilance and attention.

### 5. Monthly Summary & Cycle Review
* **Closing Cycles:** At the start of each month, the system consolidates logs, trend statistics, and purpose evolution from the previous month.
* **Renewing Commitments:** A solid foundation for recalibrating priorities and entering the next cycle with clarity of direction.

## Zero-Knowledge Privacy and Encryption Architecture

In Wingene VIDA, privacy is a cryptographic certainty rather than a mere contractual promise:

1. **Strong Symmetric Encryption (AES-256):** All records are encrypted and compressed locally before any transmission. The encryption key is derived from your master password — it is never sent or stored on external servers.
2. **Storage in your Personal Google Drive:** Encrypted files reside exclusively in a reserved, hidden folder (`appDataFolder`) inside your personal Google account. Neither the Wingene developers nor any third parties can access or decrypt your records.
3. **Anonymous AI Processing on Mobile Devices:** Text analysis requests sent to Gemini are ephemeral and strictly anonymized, adhering to commercial enterprise API terms that prohibit using customer data to train foundational AI models.
4. **PWA Security Model:** To safeguard credentials and ensure cost and data security, the Web App operates in read-only mode for generating new AI insights, ensuring sensitive API calls remain safely encapsulated within the native mobile apps.

## The VIDA Ethical-Existential Method

Developed within the Wingene philosophical project, the method provides a daily practice for character cultivation and fulfilling an authentic life (*eudaimonia*):

* **V — Values:** The compass. Which non-negotiable principles anchor your choices?
* **I — Imperfections:** The mirror of truth. Acknowledging vulnerabilities without paralyzing guilt to allow continuous realignment.
* **D — Decisions:** Motion. Transitioning from abstract contemplation into real-world action.
* **A — Attention:** The fertile ground. Mindful awareness with oneself, others, and the universal web of life.

To explore the deeper philosophical framework, read the essay [The VIDA Method: Wingene in Practice](https://blog.wingene.com.br/wingene/the-vida-method.html).
