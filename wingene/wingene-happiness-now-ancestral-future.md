### [Wingene](./)

### **Victory of the Virtues**

This essay sets out to present a worldview that began to take shape in September 1998 with the creation of a neologism: ***Wingene***, the fusion of **win** and **gene** — victory and heritage. Its meaning goes far beyond genetic triumph: it expands into the eternal human quest for the meaning of life—a profound yearning, woven in golden threads from the maternal womb.

Etymologically, while the word *family* historically originated in connection with external structures (the household, property, servants), the word *gene* was born attached to internal essence (birth, generation, the continuous flow of life). In the same vein, **win** teaches us that true victory is never the mere accident of overcoming a rival. To win is the state of joyful satisfaction (*Wunjo*) that crowns a journey of deep effort, labor, and focused desire (*Winnan*); it is drawing ever closer to what one truly loves.

From birth, human beings carry questions that stir and challenge; the perpetual drive to learn is our ancestral inheritance. Yet knowledge alone is not enough; victory is completed only in the ongoing practice of personal self-refinement — a journey that never ends, continually shaped with each step toward a utopian ideal. This refinement is the true path toward what everyone genuinely seeks: happiness. Along this way, triumphs dawn radiantly, illuminating a being who, increasingly fulfilled, flourishes in full *eudaimonia*.

*Eudaimonia*, from the Greek *eu* (well) and *daimon* (spirit), represents the flourishing of happiness — a serene and enduring state born from internal strengths. It aligns closely with Stoicism, where joy arises from within, steadfast and unshakable. Moral refinement has been debated for millennia; according to Aristotle¹, eudaimonia is the natural fruition of a virtuous life. We do not intend to reinvent this philosophical concept, but rather to use it as the cornerstone for *Wingene*. The first step toward this flourishing is self-knowledge, guiding the path of personal evolution. Despite my innate thirst for learning, it was through Logosophy that this impulse solidified into commitment, as I began to confront with deeper gravity the challenge of conscious moral improvement — the core aim of Carlos Pecotche's work².

*Wingene* rests upon naturalistic foundations. For this reason, my concept of spirit is not metaphysical: it inhabits the living body, manifests through biological processes, and shelters pure values and emotions within the mind. The powerful mental apparatus may at times appear to exceed bodily boundaries, traveling upon the waves of countless neuronal networks. Yet its substance is fundamentally material. The mind is like a dense forest, with trees rooted deeply throughout the entire organism that feel, command, and think. From roots to canopy, this forest constitutes a unified whole — a conscious, intricate system engaged in an ceaseless dance.

Wingene does not emerge from a vacuum, nor does it seek to sever ties with the past. It is an avowed heir to Aristotelian ethics, Stoic equanimity, Spinozist naturalism, and the self-refinement method of Logosophy. What it proposes is not an isolated, ahistorical morality, but a contemporary rearticulation: how to live this millennial pursuit in the light of modern biology, epigenetics, and an explicit responsibility toward our ancestral future.

Genetic heritage sparks the earliest lights of the mind. As António Damásio³ observes: *"Our brains and our minds are not tabulae rasae when we are born. Nor are they, however, fully genetically determined. The genetic shadow has a long reach, but it is not complete."* This biological endowment must gradually broaden through culture — the master key to moral refinement. In doing so, knowledge nourishes the being, offering the singular opportunity for genuine self-mastery and personal triumph.

This ethical naturalism acknowledges biological boundaries without succumbing to determinism. We are born with predispositions — temperament, aptitudes, vulnerabilities — yet culture continually shapes and sculpts them. Wingene operates precisely on this frontier: between what we inherit (*gene*) and what we consciously build (*win*). Between the genetic shadow and the cultural light.

### **Values and Action**

How, then, do we weave this evolution into daily existence? How do we translate genetic inheritance and the desire for transcendence into concrete, actionable steps? The answer lies in an acronym: **V**alues, **I**mperfections, **D**ecisions, and **A**ttention — VIDA (Life).

**Values** are the non-negotiable principles that guide every choice and action. They are the moral compass that keeps us faithful to ourselves, particularly in moments of trial. They provide the bedrock upon which an integrated life is built. Yet values can become muted, latent, obscured by flaws, or suffocated by unquestioned dogma, allowing imperfections to proliferate unchecked.

**Imperfections** are part of living: the lack of patience when playing with a young child, the fitness resolution abandoned by the third week, the defensive pride that resists acknowledging a fault. Embracing their reality prevents the obsessive pursuit of perfection — the greatest enemy of happiness. Acknowledging imperfections does not mean resigning to them; rather, it provides the target for conscious work. Perfection is utopian, but striving for the optimal is essential. Imperfections reveal which values have been compromised, enabling daily refinement, polishing rough edges, and forging character. To do so, however, requires the courage to decide to change.

**Decisions** grant us the power to take hold of the reins of our own destiny. Guided by values, we confront our imperfections. More than merely wishing for transformation, deciding is the courageous act of choosing to confront weaknesses and strive daily for self-betterment. Decisions are the catalyst for nobler attitudes, whether through cooperative harmony in the household or readiness to assist colleagues. New habits do not solidify on the first attempt; toward any meaningful goal, one must recognize that stumbling and starting anew is an intrinsic part of the process. Decisions govern our trajectory, our choices, and our evolving life.

**Attention**, singular and indispensable, accompanies the entire continuum. It is the art of residing in the present moment — noticing subtleties, listening genuinely. It means experiencing the richness and depth of the here-and-now, dismantling the routine of autopilot. Recognizing that the mind is prone to wander should never be an excuse to stop cultivating awareness at each juncture of our imperfect journey. An attentive mind not only perceives shortcomings, but also opens the portal to profound perceptual experiences. Contemplating a simple landscape can transcend into moments of unadulterated joy: serene euphoria.

Continually re-evaluating values in light of human fallibility is crucial to guiding sound decisions. For just as individuals are fallible, so too is culture. Consequently, errors frequently stem from flawed cultural premises. The foundational tenets of the scientific method and continuous learning provide clear navigation for an attentive mind.

This ethical-existential framework is detailed in depth in [The VIDA Method: Wingene in Practice](./the-vida-method.html).

### **Serene Euphoria**

When this path is walked with devotion — when values align, imperfections are met with honesty, decisions are enacted, and attention is diligently cultivated — human consciousness can encounter the extraordinary. This experience embodies eudaimonia, flourishing organically from the sustained practice of virtue.

The mind expands in instances of luminous joy, entering radiant states far beyond ordinary experience. A vast euphoria, held within serenity; an inward warmth like a flame that warms without burning, enduring amid the urban gray or in the tranquil landscape that flows from vital sap — senses and thoughts cease to disturb; consciousness simply hovers. In these moments, a state of grace dawns, where synapses pulse in heightened harmony and energy.

To touch this gift is innate to human nature — yet so few seek it, caught up in the heavy clouds of worldly obligations, bound to the eternal toil of Sisyphus: rolling the identical boulder up the hill each day. Too often, the crushing weight of this rock is amplified by modern social inequalities, transcending mere monotony to become the grim burden of the majority. Nevertheless, the intrinsic wonder of existence holds the potential to touch every human life, inviting happiness to blossom.

The mental canopy can dance among the highest treetops, opening into **serene euphoria**, where the physical body serves merely as the stage for the dance of the mind. It suffices that these steps glide across the floor, unconcerned with the audience. In the quiet refuge of home, amidst the momentum of work, or even through repetitive routines, the symphony can unfold. For the mind awaits only the cue of this delicate choreography—a **waltz of synchronized neurons**.

Serene euphoria can occur in memorable moments of tranquil emotion and unified thought. It does not demand the void of structured breathing techniques, but rather a graceful dance beyond reactive thinking and feeling. When both stage and spectators no longer disturb a mind that expands in the joyful awareness of being, freed from the clamor of external sensation: **it is simply the dance of a conscious being**. Yet this experience cannot remain static — a blossoming mind naturally yearns to expand outward through creation.

### **The Power of Creation**

Life broadens through the act of invention — from the Latin *invenire*, which literally means to discover or come upon. I first encountered this nuance while reading the biography of Jorge Luis Borges. The role of the creative artist is in no way diminished by the realization that creating is ultimately an act of uncovering; for seeking solutions remains a daring challenge. Charting unexplored destinations, navigating the living ocean of nature: creating is, at its root, the purest source of *eudaimonia* — the exhilaration of setting foot upon unknown shores. Paths to masterpieces are always discoveries of the world. The answers reside latent within the universe, waiting for creative minds to unveil them.

Physical theories are models; the ancient music of the spheres — postulated nearly three millennia ago — became elliptical with Newton and deepened further through Einsteinian relativity. The latter, in turn, yields ground to quantum mechanics in describing the music of fundamental particles — the cosmic and the infinitesimal, two descriptions coexisting side by side. A grand unified theory remains concealed, beckoning the inquisitive spirit to illuminate fresh frontiers of scientific understanding.

New philosophies still await their cartographers. Navigators of the metaphysical ocean search for answers already present upon undiscovered continents. For now, they often seem adrift, overshadowed by ever-bolder leaps in technological science and by the sea of formulaic self-help literature flooding contemporary culture.

Master artists of words and stone reveal works of enduring beauty, creations that mirror the unspeakable dance of their minds. They weave with indelible threads drawn from the physical world, striving to articulate the hidden wonders veiled from common sight. Through their labor, humanity preserves and enriches our shared cultural inheritance—a direct fruit of nature—bequeathed to generations yet to come.

Discoveries continue to unfold; our accumulated cultural heritage is the springboard that enables each leap—sometimes modest, at other times daring. *Wingene* happens each and every day, in spite of setbacks and disillusionment. Natural wisdom permeates the cosmos, awaiting inventors. At this very moment, brilliant insights and inventive breakthroughs are being nurtured across the globe — culture advancing day by day.

### **Ancestral Future**

Why dedicate ourselves to self-betterment if we will not be here to enjoy the distant future? The answer is found both in eudaimonia as the immediate, living experience of this striving and in the legacy woven by those who walked before us. Like our ancestors, we must form the bedrock upon which subsequent generations will stand, safeguarding their evolutionary path. In this way, we can envision a collective Wingene, wherein each generation enriches the soil so that the next may flourish in eudaimonia.

Indigenous philosopher Ailton Krenak⁴ profoundly notes that the future is ancestral: the living ecosystems that sustain existence precede humanity itself. Preserving this natural, ancestral future is fundamental, even if it cannot forestall the ultimate, inevitable twilight of our earthly civilization.

Our present civilization has a finite window to flourish; the human race moves inevitably toward a finite horizon. The urgency of conscious evolution is dictated by time, for the cultural sap of life will not pulse forever. Human existence will eventually pass; in due time, the broader terrestrial biosphere will also fade. We will not terraform distant galaxies in time to outrun entropy; even our silicon heirs — artificial intelligences — will eventually fall silent.

Our allotted span is measured in the few brief decades of a mortal lifetime. We have this singular opportunity to discover, create, and comprehend all that we can. Cultivating culture and refining our moral character requires no external justification. It is inscribed in our nature: the fire of Prometheus must remain tended, casting its light into the ancestral future — even if that flame must journey through total cosmic darkness before kindling again.

### **Infinite Cosmogonies**

A collective Wingene might seem inconsequential against the vast canvas of cosmic time, which renders human history a fleeting flash. How can one sustain meaning when the end of all things is inevitable? My outlook on cosmic origins has always been anchored in empirical science, yet it long yearned for an existential dimension. It was contemplating the eventual extinction of earthly life that compelled me to ask: what hope sustains our labor if the universe itself must die? The answer emerged from an audacious cosmological speculation — offered not as empirical dogma, but as a hypothesis that comforts the contemplative spirit: what if the death of one universe marks the birth of another?

This perspective echoes one of the most intriguing and radical concepts in contemporary physics, wherein a black hole may connect at its deepest threshold to a white hole. As Carlo Rovelli⁵ depicts: *"If you come with me, we will reach the boundary of the black hole's horizon, enter, plunge all the way down to where space and time dissolve, cross through, and emerge into the white hole where time is reversed, stepping out into the distant future."* Our observable universe could itself be the unfolding of a white hole. I envision an unending succession of coupled black and white holes, with life capable of arising within each new cosmic cradle. The sorrow of finitude is soothed by apprehending the cyclical eternity of this grand rhythm.

All that has blossomed upon our pale blue vessel will one day be stardust and ashes. We will not live again, nor will we return on another stage. Our timeframe is the present one: measured in breaths, calibrated to the heartbeat. Where will all this beauty go — the vibrant tapestries of nature and human feeling? This certain end brings me no despair, for I gaze into the depths of massive stellar collapse and perceive dark gateways into invisible universes — unfolding new spectra of light, hosting other lives.

There endures an optimistic hope: infinite cosmogonies awaiting future creators to discover unheard symphonies — explorers of new worlds, heralds of a *Wingene* that never ends. The very same essence that animates our brief existence, echoing movements of a melody that resounds throughout eternity — a movement within a finite existence that keeps vibrating within the endless life of the cosmos.

***

The victory of the virtues is sculpted through daily dedication. Only through this ongoing effort can one experience serene euphoria in the present. Passing down this legacy, however, is equally an enduring fountain of eudaimonia — happiness now, ancestral future.

*Wingene* realizes itself across intertwined dimensions: in the personal effort to evolve, in the serene euphoria that elevates awareness, in the creative power to discover, in the conscious legacy bequeathed to tomorrow's generations, and in the cosmic hope of emerging universes. Every mindful act conducts the cadence of life: decisions overcoming imperfections, values molding character, an individual existence composing phrases within a vast symphony. May our brief passage be far more than a passive transit — may it be harmonious measures that resonate in the universal music of existence.
